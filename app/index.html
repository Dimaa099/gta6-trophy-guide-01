0% to the Platinum

    The order to earn them in, built from the official descriptions: story first, then side characters, then the map, then races and shooting, then robberies, then money and the 100% push. PowerPyx rates the whole run about 200 hours at difficulty 4/10, with the story alone taking 50–60 hours.

    Only one trophy is flagged as possibly missable by any published guide. Everything else can be earned after the story, in free roam — but a manual save before a final mission costs nothing, so the habit below is worth keeping.

    The save habit

    Story missions cannot be replayed, so keep a manual save before any mission that looks like a one-off — especially the final mission of a side character's strand (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone) and the vague story moments (Personal Best, Actions Speak Louder, Bad Luck, I Guess, The Truth Is Out There). The game gives you 15 manual save slots; use them. No other trophy is confirmed missable by any published guide.

    Basis: PowerPyx and GTABase flag Positive Mental Attitude (via FullSweep and GTA Zone); GTAViceCityHub reports no missable trophy confirmed and notes the 15 manual save slots.
